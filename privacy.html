Privacy Policy — Vartex Search

Vartex Engineering · Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Vartex Engineering ("we", "our", or "us") built Vartex Search as a desktop document indexing and search utility for Windows 10 and Windows 11. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles local files, optional Google Drive synchronization, and user data.

1. Zero Telemetry & Local-First Processing

Vartex Search does not collect, transmit, sell, or share analytics, usage telemetry, crash reports, or document contents with Vartex Engineering or any third-party analytics service. All document parsing, SQLite FTS5 indexing, and search queries execute 100% locally on your Windows device.

2. Local Storage Location

The search index database and optional user configuration files are stored strictly inside your local Windows user profile at:

%LOCALAPPDATA%\VartexEngineering\VartexSearch\search_index.db

You may delete this folder at any time to permanently erase all indexed document text and local tokens from your computer.

3. Optional Google Drive Integration (Read-Only)

If you choose to click Sync Google Docs, the application requests read-only access to your Google Drive using the restricted scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly.

  • Purpose: Exports text from your Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Slides solely to build your local full-text search index on your device.
  • No Modification: The application never edits, deletes, shares, or uploads files to your Google Drive.
  • Token Storage: Your OAuth refresh/access token is stored locally on your machine in token.json inside %LOCALAPPDATA%\VartexEngineering\VartexSearch.
  • Credentials Handling: Users provide their own Google Cloud Desktop OAuth client file (credentials.json) configured by their administrator, and authorize access individually via Google OAuth 2.0 consent.
  • Google API Limited Use Compliance: Vartex Search's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Local Desktop Integrations

When you click Open at Exact Location, the application uses standard Windows desktop APIs (Microsoft Office COM automation for Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, or Microsoft Edge / Google Chrome for PDF page anchors) locally on your machine to open the selected document directly at the matched location.

5. Data Deletion & Revoking Access

  • To delete your local search index and Google Drive token, delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\VartexEngineering\VartexSearch.
  • To revoke Google Drive access at any time, visit your Google Account Third-Party Access page and remove the app.
